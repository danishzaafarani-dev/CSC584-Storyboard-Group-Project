# Campus Bazaar — Business Rules and Implementation Notes

This document defines the validation rules that complement the relational schema.

## Account and access
- Student ID and email must be unique.
- Only ACTIVE (and, where required, verified) accounts may create listings or transact.
- Store only password hashes generated by a suitable password-hashing algorithm.
- Listing owners can edit/deactivate their own items. Moderation actions require administrator authorization.

## Listings
- Each item belongs to one seller and one category.
- Item price cannot be negative; quantity must be at least one.
- An item can have multiple images; at most one should be marked as the primary image by application logic.
- Only AVAILABLE items should appear in normal marketplace search results.

## Purchase and exchange
- The seller is `Item.seller_id`; do not accept a client-supplied seller identity independently.
- The buyer cannot be the seller of the requested item.
- PURCHASE requires `offered_item_id = NULL`.
- EXCHANGE requires an offered item owned by the buyer and currently available.
- Use a database transaction and row locking when accepting a request so two sellers/actions cannot reserve the same item concurrently.
- A seller may accept or reject a request for their own item only.
- On acceptance, reserve the requested item and (for exchange) the offered item.
- On completion, require confirmation from both buyer and seller. Then mark the requested item SOLD or EXCHANGED and the offered item EXCHANGED.
- On rejection/cancellation, release any reservation when no other accepted transaction exists.

## Messaging and notifications
- Sender and receiver must be different users.
- A message may be associated with a transaction, but general pre-transaction messages may have no transaction ID.
- Notify the recipient when a new message or relevant transaction status change occurs.

## Reviews
- Reviews are allowed only after a transaction is COMPLETED.
- Reviewer and reviewee must be the buyer and seller participants in that transaction.
- Each participant may submit at most one review per transaction.
- Rating must be an integer from 1 through 5.

## Reports and moderation
- A report must target exactly one user or one item.
- Only authorized moderators may change report status or suspend accounts.
- Keep report history for audit purposes; avoid hard-deleting records needed for moderation.

## Recommended future schema enhancement
For strict two-party completion confirmation, create a separate `Transaction_Confirmation` table with `confirmation_id` (PK), `transaction_id` (FK), `user_id` (FK), `confirmed_at`, and a unique constraint on `(transaction_id, user_id)`. This is preferable to storing two loosely coupled boolean fields if the system later needs an audit trail.
