Campus Bazaar user flow

An expanded student journey covering listings, communication, transactions, reviews and reporting. The added functions are proposed backend features.

1. Login / Register

Student signs in or registers. System validates and opens the marketplace.

2. Homepage

Student browses categories and listings.

3. Search / Filter

Student searches by name or category. System shows matching items.

4. Item details

Description, condition, price and seller information.

5. Create listing

Seller submits details, category, condition, price and image.

6. Purchase / Exchange request

Buyer requests purchase or offers an item. System creates a PENDING transaction.

7. Manage request

Seller accepts or rejects. System updates the status.

8. Manage request

Seller accepts or rejects the request. System updates status and sends a notification.

9. Complete transaction

Buyer and seller meet on campus and confirm completion.

10. Review

Participants leave a rating after a completed transaction.

11. Wishlist & messages

Students save listings and communicate with sellers.

12. Report & moderation

Students report inappropriate listings or users for moderator review.
